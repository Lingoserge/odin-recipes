Homemade cookies

Homemade Cookies Ingredients.

These are ingredients for homemade cookies.

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup chocolate chips

Steps to make homemade cookies

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. In a bowl, cream together the butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar until light and fluffy.
  3. Beat in the egg and vanilla extract.
  4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt.
  5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix until just combined.
  6. Stir in the chocolate chips.
  7. Drop rounded tablespoons of dough onto an ungreased baking sheet.
  8. Bake for 9-11 minutes, or until the edges are golden brown.
  9. Let cool on the baking sheet for a few minutes before moving to a wire rack to cool completely.
