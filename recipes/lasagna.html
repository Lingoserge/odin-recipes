Homemade Lasagna Ingredients

These are the ingredients that you can use to make your homemade lasagna

  • Meat.
  • Onions.
  • Tomatoes.
  • sugar.
  • Spices and Seasonings.
  • Noodles.
  • Cheese.
  • Eggs.

Steps to make a homemade lasagna

  1. Cook the meat.
  2. Cook the noodles.
  3. Make the cheese layer.
  4. Assemble the lasagna.
  5. Bake the lasagna.
