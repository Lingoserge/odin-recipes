Homemade Pizza

Homemade pizza ingredients.

These are the ingredients required to make a homemade pizza.

  • 1 pre-made pizza crust
  • 1 cup of pizza sauce
  • 2 cups of shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup of sliced pepperoni
  • 1/4 cup of chopped bell peppers

Steps to make a homemade pizza.

  1. Preheat your oven to the temperature.
  2. Spread the pizza sauce evenly over the crust.
  3. Sprinkle the mozarella cheese over the sauce.
  4. Add your peperonni and bell peppers on top of the cheese.
  5. Bake for 15-20 minutes,or until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly.
